# Keep Product Notes Live Across Trip History

Product notes are current household guidance rather than historical facts, so every Planned and Completed Trip resolves the note from the Product while Product names and Departments snapshot at completion. This sacrifices point-in-time note fidelity in exchange for one consistent reusable instruction that cannot diverge across Trip Entries.

## Consequences

- Editing a Product note changes the note shown in past Completed Trips.
- A Completed Trip cannot establish which Product note was visible when shopping occurred.
